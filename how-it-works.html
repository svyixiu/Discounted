How Discounted works

One catalog for Steam discounts.

Discounted collects Steam sale prices in one place so you can compare them quickly. Steam is always the final word on price.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Discounted games come from Steam’s specials listings. DLC, bundles and named sale events are researched by hand, with the offer end date when Steam lists one.

  2. 02

    Record

    Each listing keeps its price, discount and the source it was checked against. The product page shows that source and the date the catalog was updated.

  3. 03

    Filter

    Narrow by type, genre, discount and budget, or browse a sale event. Switch currencies to see estimated prices; conversions use current exchange rates.

  4. 04

    Buy on Steam

    Open the deal on Steam to confirm the price, region, requirements and offer details. Discounted doesn’t sell games or earn from purchases.

What the popularity labels mean

Steam reviews
The total number of user reviews on Steam. It’s a sign of how widely a game is known, not a count of copies sold.
Top seller
The game currently appears on Steam’s top-sellers chart, which Steam ranks by revenue. Discounted doesn’t estimate or publish sales numbers.
Featured
Featured lists deals from named sale events first, then everything else by the size of the discount. It isn’t paid placement.
