FROM THE GROUND TO YOUR LIBRARY

Less digging.
More discovering.

01

Dig

The good stuff, unearthed.

We bring discounted Steam games, DLC and bundles into one catalog. Every listing comes from the current sale snapshot, so you can browse across promotions in one place.

02

Verify

Know what you’re looking at.

Each gem keeps its source in “verified by”, along with the listed offer end date. Some offers are grounded in a sale event; others are checked on a Steam offer page. The product page tells you which.

03

Sort

Find your kind of rare.

Quartz, Sapphire, Amethyst, Topaz, Prism. Deeper discounts mean rarer gems. Narrow by product type, budget and rarity, or switch currencies to compare estimated prices.

04

Play

Your next stop: Steam.

Open the deal on Steam to check the current price, region, requirements and offer details before buying. Discounted does not sell games. Steam prices and promotions can change.
