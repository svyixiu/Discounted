How it works

No digging.

Discounted organizes the Steam promotions in the current sale snapshot so you can narrow the list by product type, budget, currency and ordering without working through several store pages.

01

Choose what you want.

Browse games, DLC, bundles, or everything together. Full view emphasizes artwork; Compact view is built for scanning a large list quickly.

02

Set the budget.

The dual range control filters by the sale price. Changing currency converts both the displayed prices and the budget range.

03

Open the deal.

Use View Here for a focused Discounted page with related recommendations, or open Steam directly from the catalog.

04

Prices are a snapshot.

Steam can change or end promotions. Discounted reflects the sale data collected for the current snapshot rather than claiming a permanent price.
