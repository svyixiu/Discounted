## Coverage and sources

Scanned every offset in Steam's `IStoreQueryService/Query/v1` discounted US index in 109 pages, with alphabetical sorting and a minimum discount of 1%. The initial scan returned 108,521 raw rows and 108,520 unique identities. The live index population changed from 108,520 to 108,521 during collection, with one repeated identity. A final 115-page scan used 50-row overlaps to avoid gaps caused by changing page boundaries. It found 108,569 unique identities, exactly matching Steam's stable reported population for that pass, and 55 additional IDs. New and disappeared IDs were independently reverified. All page requests completed. Expanded related purchase options, verified 42,253 initial bundle/package candidates plus supplemental editions and newly discovered options, checked every old identified product, and made 205 title searches. Request URLs, server dates, capture times and response hashes are in `2026-10-05-evidence.json`.

Current site categories: bundle: 41,940, dlc: 35,153, game: 65,057, other: 7,571. Soundtracks, software and other app types use the existing `other` category. Expansions, season passes and content packs use `dlc`; editions, upgrades, collections, franchise/publisher bundles and packages use their real Steam app/bundle/package identity.

## Data corrections

- Consolidated records by Steam identity rather than title spelling. Corrected the Fallen Order Deluxe Edition identity instead of sharing the base game's app ID; consolidated the duplicate LIGHTNING RETURNS record.
- Corrected zero original-price records for QuiVr and Trainz Railroad Simulator 2019. Used standalone app purchase options, so cheaper bundles do not supply misleading base-game prices.
- Excluded 108,582 standalone purchase-package aliases from additional listings. Distinct editions and bundles remain individually addressable.
- Replaced stale prices, percentages, provenance and promotion deadlines with current Steam quotes. Removed expired promotion names unless the existing verified deadline still matched.
- Recorded end times in UTC from Steam's active discounts. For bundles with multiple timed components, the deadline is the earliest component offer change. Prices include Steam's bundle savings baseline and can change with ownership.
- All active rows have a canonical Steam ID, valid positive original price, lower sale price, a 1–100% discount and a clean Steam URL. No active row needs a title-search fallback.

This is the complete change ledger; `games.json` holds current prices for every addition.

## Uncertain items

26 old rows could not be verified as a current offer. They are preserved in the change ledger and removed from the active catalog. `off_sale` means a successful current Steam quote with no discount; `unavailable` means Steam did not expose an available product; other reasons identify missing identity, individual price, inconsistent quote, currency or expiry. An expired historical date alone was not treated as proof of off-sale status.

A bundle may continue to offer permanent savings after a component sale ends; its timestamp describes when the recorded price can first change. Quotes are USD for an unowned basket; users should confirm their local price and ownership-adjusted basket on Steam.

The discovery API returned zero direct bundle-index results when queried for bundles alone. Bundle coverage comes from purchase options exposed by all discovered apps and the old curated catalog; a hidden or unlinked bundle may remain undiscovered. No claim is made that every possible Steam bundle has been indexed.

## Validation

The scan and final reconciliation had no failed API pages. JSON parsing, old/new count reconciliation, Steam ID uniqueness, route uniqueness, schema fields, USD quotes, price order, discount arithmetic and future recorded deadlines are checked before publishing. Independent Steam store-page spot checks cover Cyberpunk 2077, Phantom Liberty, Fallen Order, LIGHTNING RETURNS, QuiVr, Trainz 2019 and the Cyberpunk Ultimate Edition bundle. The live index changes during collection, so the evidence gives capture times and population drift rather than claiming an atomic snapshot. The interface uses the existing schema and route helpers.
